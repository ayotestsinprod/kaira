FAQ

Common questions

Is a will created with Kaira legally valid?

Yes. Wills created through Kaira follow the requirements of the Wills Act 1837. You'll need two witnesses to sign, just like any solicitor-drafted will. We guide you through every step.

How does Kaira find my old pensions and accounts?

Our AI agents use your employment history, financial connections, and public records to trace dormant pensions, forgotten accounts, and unclaimed assets. You verify everything before it's added to your estate map.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. We use bank-grade encryption (AES-256), are FCA-registered, and never sell your data. Your information is stored in UK data centres and you can delete your account at any time.

What happens to my data if Kaira shuts down?

Your estate plan is always exportable as a PDF. We also partner with a regulated trust company to ensure continuity of access for your beneficiaries, regardless of what happens to us.

Can Kaira handle crypto and digital assets?

Yes — it's one of our core strengths. We support multi-chain wallet tracking, secure key documentation, and clear instructions for beneficiaries on how to access digital assets.
